Ask your AI agent what's on your Moodle.

A few commands for your AI agent that read Moodle for you: what's due, what a brief is actually asking for, and what to read before your next seminar. Read-only, built for UCL.

Paste this into your AI agent

Install https://github.com/Forrest404/read-moodle-skill for me. Read SETUP.md and follow it step by step.

Needs a Mac and an AI agent that can run commands on it, such as Claude Code or Codex. The agent does the setup; you log in to Moodle once.

Commands

I made these because I was opening four module pages every week just to work out what was due and what to read. You can use the commands, or just ask in plain English, like "what do I need to read before Thursday?" Some agents start commands with $ instead of /, for example $today in Codex.

/todayWhat's due, this week's sessions and readings, and new announcements.
/deadlinesEverything due in the next two weeks, and whether it's submitted.
/brief <name>Reads the brief and marking criteria and tells you what to hand in.
/module <code> week 7One module's week, with slides and readings.
/gradesYour marks and a summary of the feedback.
/planBreaks deadlines into steps and adds them to your calendar once you've checked them.
/assignmentsEvery assignment with dates and status.
/ask <question>Anything else on Moodle.

How it works

Moodle has an API that its mobile app uses. During setup you log in once in your browser, and a small helper saves the key Moodle gives back. After that, a script on your Mac fetches your deadlines, pages and files, and your AI agent turns them into a proper answer.

Moodlecourses, briefs, grades
→
Your Macmoodle.py and your key in Keychain
→
Your AI agentreads it and answers you

It never sees your password, and the script refuses anything that would change something on Moodle. That's enforced in the code, not just in the instructions.

What it can access

Reads from Moodle

  • Courses and week pages
  • Deadlines and submission status
  • Briefs, slides and files
  • Announcements
  • Grades and feedback

Never

  • Submits or uploads
  • Posts or replies
  • Marks anything as done
  • Changes settings
  • Asks for your password

Adds to your Mac

  • ~/read-moodle-skill
  • The commands, in your agent's skills folder
  • MoodleKey.app, built locally
  • Your Moodle key, in Keychain

Notion and your calendar are only used if you set them up. No servers, accounts or tracking.

Before you install

  1. I've only tested it on UCL Moodle. Other Moodle sites may work if they allow the Moodle app.
  2. It's Mac only for now.
  3. Chat apps like the ChatGPT or Claude websites can't run it, because they can't run anything on your Mac. You need an agent that can, such as Claude Code or Codex.
  4. If UCL turns off access for the Moodle app, this stops working too.

Help

If something breaks

Run python3 -I ~/read-moodle-skill/moodle.py doctor. It checks each part and tells you what to fix. After a password change, run it with login instead.

Update or remove

Update with git pull && ./install.sh in the folder. Remove with ./uninstall.sh, then delete the folder.

Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub.

Useful?

If it saved you some clicking, give it a star on GitHub. It's free, takes a second, and helps other students find it.
